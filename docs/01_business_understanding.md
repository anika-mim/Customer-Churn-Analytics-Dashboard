# Phase 1: Business Understanding

| Item | Definition |
|---|---|
| Business objective | Reduce customer churn and protect recurring revenue |
| Analysis approach | Measure churn, compare customer segments, identify risk drivers, and estimate revenue exposure |
| Business value | Helps management focus retention effort on customers and behaviors with the greatest financial impact |
| Expected insight | Churn will be concentrated in a small number of actionable customer, contract, billing, and experience segments |

## Business Problem Statement

The company is experiencing customer churn but lacks a single view of who is leaving, why they leave, how much revenue is lost, and which active customers need immediate retention attention.

The dashboard should answer these questions without requiring management to review customer-level spreadsheets.

## Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Main Need |
|---|---|
| Executive leadership | Overall churn, retention, revenue loss, and recommended action |
| Customer success | List of high-risk customers for proactive outreach |
| Marketing | Customer segments for targeted retention campaigns |
| Finance | Annual revenue lost and annual revenue at risk |
| Product and service teams | Plans, support issues, and satisfaction patterns linked with churn |
| Data analyst | Reliable model, definitions, measures, and repeatable reporting |

## KPI Definitions

| KPI | Definition | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| Total Customers | Distinct customers in the dataset | Provides the population size |
| Active Customers | Customers whose current status is Active | Shows the retained customer base |
| Churned Customers | Customers whose current status is Churned | Shows customer loss |
| Churn Rate | Churned customers divided by total customers | Main customer-loss indicator |
| Retention Rate | Active customers divided by total customers | Shows the share of customers retained |
| Revenue Lost | Annualized monthly charges of churned customers | Estimates recurring revenue no longer retained |
| Average Customer Value | Historical revenue divided by customers | Describes average realized customer value |
| Customer Lifetime Value | Average monthly revenue per customer multiplied by average tenure | Simple portfolio estimate of customer value |
| Monthly Churn Rate | Customers churned during a month divided by customers active at the start of the month | Tracks churn over time more fairly than counts |
| Revenue at Risk | Annualized monthly charges of active high-risk customers | Prioritizes retention by financial exposure |

## Business Questions

1. What is the current churn and retention position?
2. How much annual recurring revenue has been lost?
3. Which demographic segments have the highest churn?
4. Which customer groups generate the highest revenue?
5. Which contract types and plans are associated with churn?
6. Which payment behaviors are associated with churn?
7. Does churn occur more often among short-tenure customers?
8. How do satisfaction and support activity relate to churn?
9. Which active customers are high risk?
10. What practical retention actions should management prioritize?

## Scope

This is a descriptive and diagnostic BI project. The risk score is a transparent rule-based score for prioritization, not a machine-learning prediction. That keeps the project explainable and appropriate for an entry-level portfolio.
