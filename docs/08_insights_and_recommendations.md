# Key Insights and Recommendations

## Executive Findings

### 1. Churn is financially meaningful

- 385 of 1,500 customers have churned.
- Overall churn is 25.7%.
- Estimated annual recurring revenue lost is $339,078.
- 125 active high-risk customers represent another $105,230 in annual revenue exposure.

**Business value:** Retention can be prioritized using both customer risk and revenue impact.

### 2. Contract flexibility is linked with higher churn

- Month-to-month churn: 32.7%
- One-year churn: 18.8%
- Two-year churn: 15.7%

**Expected insight:** Longer commitments are associated with stronger retention.

**Recommendation:** Offer a targeted annual-contract incentive to stable month-to-month customers rather than applying a blanket discount.

### 3. Billing behavior is actionable

- Electronic check churn: 37.2%
- Credit card churn: 19.7%
- No-autopay churn: 32.0%
- Autopay churn: 19.2%

**Expected insight:** Payment friction and manual payment behavior are strong retention signals.

**Recommendation:** Promote autopay, review electronic-check failures, and send payment reminders before due dates.

### 4. Customer experience is a major warning signal

- Satisfaction score 1 churn: 52.6%
- Satisfaction score 2 churn: 55.8%
- Satisfaction score 5 churn: 15.2%
- Four or more support calls churn: 40.1%

**Expected insight:** Low satisfaction and repeated support demand identify customers who need service recovery.

**Recommendation:** Trigger a customer-success case when satisfaction falls below 3 or support calls reach 4.

### 5. Early tenure requires attention

- 0-12 month churn: 57.0%
- 13-24 month churn: 21.2%
- 25-36 month churn: 8.3%
- 37+ month churn: 2.0%

This pattern is partly influenced by how tenure is measured for customers who have already churned, but it still shows that churn is concentrated early in the relationship.

**Recommendation:** Build a structured first-year onboarding program with 30-, 60-, 90-, and 180-day engagement checks.

### 6. Subscription plans have different risk and value profiles

- Basic plan churn: 31.9%
- Premium plan churn: 25.6%
- Standard plan churn: 21.0%
- Standard plan historical revenue: approximately $1.13 million

**Expected insight:** The Standard plan combines the lowest plan churn with the highest total historical revenue.

**Recommendation:** Study the Standard plan experience and use it as the benchmark for Basic plan improvements.

## Priority Action Plan

| Priority | Action | Owner | Success Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Contact active high-risk customers | Customer Success | Revenue at risk retained |
| 2 | Launch early-life retention journey | Customer Success and Marketing | 0-12 month churn rate |
| 3 | Promote autopay enrollment | Billing and Marketing | Autopay adoption and churn |
| 4 | Offer annual-contract conversion | Marketing and Finance | Month-to-month conversion |
| 5 | Escalate low-satisfaction cases | Service Operations | Churn after service recovery |

## Important Interpretation Note

These findings show association, not proof of causation. A real company should test retention offers with controlled pilots and compare churn before and after the intervention.
